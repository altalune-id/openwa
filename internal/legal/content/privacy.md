This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Altalune collects when you use OpenWA (the "Service"), why, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It applies together with the Terms of Service.

In short: we collect what we need to run your account and your projects, we process message content only to provide the Service to you, we never use it for advertising or model training, and it is deleted on the schedule you set.

## 1. Controller and contact

Altalune, operating as altalune.id, is the controller of the account and usage data described below. For message content and contacts that you process through the Service, see section 4. Contact us about privacy at legal@altalune.id.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account data:** your email address, display name, preferred language, the identity provider you signed in with and its user identifier, when you accepted the Terms, and your organization and project memberships and roles.
- **Linked WhatsApp account data:** the phone number, WhatsApp identifiers (JID and LID), display name and device platform of each account you link, and its connection status.
- **Message data:** the content and metadata of messages sent and received through linked accounts, chat and group names, and delivery and read status.
- **Contacts:** names, phone numbers and WhatsApp identifiers of people your linked accounts exchange messages with.
- **Media:** metadata about media files (type, size, file name) and the keys needed to fetch them from WhatsApp when you open them; media is fetched on demand and not stored by us in this version.
- **Integration data:** API key names, scopes and usage times (key secrets are stored only as hashes), webhook endpoint addresses and delivery logs (signing secrets are stored encrypted).
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser user agent, request paths, request identifiers and timestamps in our logs, and the cookies needed for your session, your language choice and one-time notices. Your theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.

## 3. Why and on what basis

- To provide the Service you asked for: creating your account, linking WhatsApp accounts, sending and receiving messages, delivering webhooks. Basis: performance of our contract with you.
- To keep the Service secure and to prevent abuse, including detecting spam and unauthorized access. Basis: our legitimate interests and our legal obligations.
- To communicate with you about the Service, including changes to these documents. Basis: performance of the contract.
- To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests. Basis: legal obligation.

Where a law such as Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) requires consent for a specific processing activity, we ask for it and you may withdraw it at any time.

## 4. Processing on your behalf

For message content, contacts and media that you process through the Service, you (or your organization) decide why and how that data is processed, and we process it on your behalf. We commit to the following:

- we process it only to provide the Service to you and to keep it secure;
- we keep message history only as long as your project's retention setting allows, then delete it;
- we never use it for advertising, profiling for marketing, or training machine-learning models;
- we delete it when you delete the device or project it belongs to;
- our staff access it only when needed to operate, secure or support the Service.

We do not offer a separate data processing agreement in this version; this section is our commitment. You are responsible for having a lawful basis, and the consent the law requires, for the people whose messages and contacts you process.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only:

- with WhatsApp, which necessarily receives the messages you send through linked accounts;
- with the webhook endpoints you configure, which receive the events you subscribe to;
- with service providers who host our infrastructure, deliver email or provide sign-in, under confidentiality obligations and only for those purposes;
- when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or Altalune;
- with a successor entity if the Service is reorganized, merged or sold, subject to this policy.

## 6. International transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in Indonesia and in other countries where our hosting and service providers operate. When data leaves Indonesia we take the steps applicable law requires so that it keeps an equivalent level of protection.

## 7. Retention

- **Account data:** while your account exists, plus 30 days after deletion so accidental deletions can be reversed and backups can expire.
- **Messages:** per your project's retention setting, 30 days by default and adjustable from 1 to 365 days; deleted earlier when the device or project is deleted.
- **Logs:** 90 days.

We may keep limited data longer where the law requires it or to resolve a dispute.

## 8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to access, correct, delete and receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent you have given. Many of these you can do yourself in the console, for example deleting devices and projects. For anything else, email legal@altalune.id from the address on your account. We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

If you are a person whose messages were processed by one of our users, contact that user first; we will help them respond.

## 9. Security measures

We protect data with encryption in transit (TLS), encryption of stored secrets, hashing of API keys, database-level isolation between organizations, least-privilege access for staff, signed session cookies, and logging of security-relevant events. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your personal data, we notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 10. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18 years old, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data as account holders. If you believe a minor has an account, contact legal@altalune.id and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. Each version carries the date at the top of this page. When we make a material change we will notify you in the console.

## 12. Contact

Privacy questions and requests go to legal@altalune.id.
